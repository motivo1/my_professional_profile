### DAY 17 — Tuesday | LEAD MAGNET | Curiosity Gap

Nobody publishes the real WooCommerce → Odoo migration checklist.

The ones that exist skip exactly the parts where implementations actually fail.

**The 10 things that sink WooCommerce → Odoo migrations:**

1. **SKU inconsistencies** — WooCommerce allows messy product codes. Odoo enforces unique SKUs. You'll find duplicates you didn't know you had.

2. **Customer duplicates** — Most WooCommerce stores have 20–35% duplicate customer rate. Migrate them as-is and your CRM is immediately broken.

3. **Order history format** — WooCommerce statuses don't map 1:1 to Odoo. Decide your mapping rules before migration, not during.

4. **Product variants** — Variable products in WooCommerce are structured differently in Odoo. Plan attribute mapping before touching the data.

5. **Images and media** — Never include product images in the main data migration. Run them separately. This causes 80% of migration timeouts.

6. **Tax configurations** — WooCommerce tax settings and Odoo fiscal positions are completely different. Map manually or your invoicing will be wrong.

7. **Supplier data** — Usually doesn't exist in WooCommerce at all. Must be built from scratch or imported from wherever it actually lives (usually spreadsheets or email).

8. **Pricing structure** — WooCommerce pricing is flat. Odoo supports pricelists and volume discounts. Decide your pricing strategy before migration.

9. **Test with 10% first** — Always. Find the edge cases before they break a full migration.

10. **Parallel run** — Keep WooCommerce live for 2 weeks after Odoo go-live. Compare daily. Switch fully only when numbers match.
